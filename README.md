# ClarityCare Suicide Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This Power BI project analyses global suicide incidence across age groups, sex, generations, and countries.  
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of demographic risk patterns and highlights key influencers that contribute to increases in suicide numbers.  
The goal is to support data-driven public health insights and inform prevention strategies.

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Total suicide incidence:** 6.75M across 101 countries.
- **Highest-risk generation:** Boomers, accounting for 35.14% of total suicides.
- **Sex differences:** Males recorded 5.19M suicides compared to 1.56M among females.
- **Age group trends:** Highest incidence among individuals aged **35–54 years** (1.95M).
- **Key influencers:**  
  - Being **male** increases suicide numbers by ~254.  
  - Being in the **Boomer generation** increases suicide numbers by ~222.

## 🧠 Features of the Dashboard
- **Demographic breakdowns** by age, sex, and generation.
- **Key Influencer analysis** to identify drivers of high suicide numbers.
- **Country-level metrics** including GDP per capita and total suicides.
- **Trend analysis** across years.
- **Interactive slicers** for filtering by sex, generation, and metrics.
